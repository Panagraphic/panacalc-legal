Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Panagraphic® ("we") developed PanaCalc as a freemium application with in-app purchases. This page describes how we collect and use user information.

Data collected by third parties

PanaCalc uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect the device advertising identifier (IDFA), subject to consent via the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt, anonymous usage data, and device information. For more information: policies.google.com/privacy.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple via StoreKit. Panagraphic® does not collect or store any payment data.

Network connections

PanaCalc connects to external servers exclusively to download public data, without transmitting any personal user data:

  • Exchange rates: Frankfurter servers (api.frankfurter.app) and, if unavailable, ER-API (open.er-api.com), to update European Central Bank exchange rates. This occurs at most once per day.
  • Market data: a Cloudflare Worker service operated by Panagraphic® and the World Bank's public APIs (api.worldbank.org), used to display benchmark indices and inflation data in the investment calculators.

No personal user data is transmitted in any of these connections.

Locally stored data

Calculation history and app preferences are stored exclusively on the device and are never transmitted to external servers.

Data NOT collected

We do not require registration or an account. We do not collect name, email, or personal data. We do not share data with third parties other than Google AdMob and Apple StoreKit.

Tracking consent (ATT)

On iOS 14 and later, the app requests tracking consent via App Tracking Transparency. If declined, non-personalized ads will be shown instead.

Children

PanaCalc is not intended for children under the age of 9. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If a parent or guardian believes their child has provided personal data through third-party services integrated into the app, they may contact us to report it.

GDPR compliance

PanaCalc does not collect or process users' personal data. Any data that may be collected is handled by third parties (Google AdMob and Apple StoreKit) according to their respective privacy policies. For any privacy-related questions, contact support@panagraphic.it.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
